# Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the iPerf app for iPhone and iPad.

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal data through the app and do not operate an analytics or tracking service in it. Network tests exchange data directly with the iPerf 3 server you choose. We recommend [running your own iPerf 3 server](https://github.com/ndfred/iperf-ios/blob/master/Documentation/Help.md).

When running a test:

* the app sends test parameters and its iPerf version to the server
* it sends or receives generated test traffic
* it exchanges test results, including transferred byte counts, elapsed time, CPU utilisation and applicable network statistics

The server can see your connection's IP address and the information exchanged during the test. Its operator may log this information; we do not control servers operated by others. Test traffic is generated by the app or the selected iPerf server; it is not taken from your personal files.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the last successful server address and port in its local settings so you do not need to enter them again. It does not maintain a saved history of test results.

## Local network access

Local network access is required to test against iPerf servers on your network. The app requests this permission when needed. If access is denied, local tests cannot connect. You can change this permission in iOS Settings.

## Help and external links

The help and privacy policy are bundled with the app and can be read without connecting to a website. If you follow an external link, it opens in your browser and the destination website's privacy policy applies.

## Analytics

The app does not include advertising, tracking or a separate analytics service. Performance statistics exchanged with your chosen server are part of the iPerf test itself.

## Your Consent

By using this app, you consent to our privacy policy.
